Mr. Fraser moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 54, An Act to amend the Workplace Safety and Insurance Act, 1997 / Projet de loi 54, Loi modifiant la Loi de 1997 sur la sécurité professionnelle et l’assurance contre les accidents du travail.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

I’ll invite the member to briefly explain his bill.

The bill makes developmental service workers, personal support workers and youth workers who are doing the same work as other schedule 1 employees part of schedule 1, because right now, in Ontario, there are people who are doing the same work without the benefit of the same protections.

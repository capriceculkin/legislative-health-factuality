Mr Lalonde moved first reading of the following bill: An Act to amend the Ambulance Act to provide for the minimum staffing and equipping of ambulance stations / Loi modifiant la Loi sur les ambulances pour assurer la dotation minimale en personnel et en équipement des postes d'ambulances.

Mr Lalonde has introduced a bill to amend the Ambulance Act. Is it the pleasure of the House the motion carry? It is carried. Does the member have a brief statement?

The bill amends the Ambulance Act by adding a new section 19(1). The new section requires the operator of a land ambulance service to ensure that for at least 12 hours each day, at least one ambulance and at least two people qualified to staff the ambulance are either located in the ambulance station or are providing ambulance services outside of the station. The 12 hours need not be consecutive.

Congressional Record, Volume 141 Issue 108 (Thursday, June 29, 1995)

REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT

ON H.R. 483, MEDICARE SELECT POLICIES

Ms. PRYCE, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 104-169) on the resolution (H. Res. 180) waiving points of order against the conference report accompanying the bill (H.R. 483) to amend title XVIII of the Social Security Act to permit Medicare select policies to be offered in all States, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

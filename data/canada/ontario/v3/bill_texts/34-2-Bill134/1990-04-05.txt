Mr Allen moved first reading of Bill 134, An Act to regulate the Sale of Tobacco.

The House divided on Mr Allen’s motion, which was agreed to on the following vote:

Allen, Beer, Bryden, Carrothers, Charlton, Collins, Cordiano, Curling, Daigeler, Dietsch, Elston, Faubert, Fulton, Furlong, Grier, Hošek, Keyes, Kormos, Laughren, LeBourdais, Leone, Lipsett, Lupusella; Mackenzie, Mahoney, Matrundola, McClelland, Morin-Strom, Oddie Munro, Pelissero, Philip, E., Phillips, G., Pouliot, Roberts, Smith, E. J., Sola, Stoner, Sullivan, Tatham, Wong.

This bill proposes to prohibit the sale of tobacco directly or indirectly unless the vendor is licensed either in sales across the counter or through a vending machine. It also requires that the preceding act, namely the Minors’ Protection Act, be repealed and it significantly increase these fines pertaining to the offence described in the act.

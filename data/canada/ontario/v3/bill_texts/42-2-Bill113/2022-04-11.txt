Ms. Stiles moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 113, An Act respecting children, youth and young adults and the COVID-19 pandemic recovery / Projet de loi 113, Loi concernant les enfants, les jeunes et les jeunes adultes, et la relance après la pandémie de COVID-19.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

I’ll invite the member for Davenport to briefly explain her bill.

The Centering Youth in Pandemic Recovery Act was first tabled in this place nearly a year ago, and regrettably the need to address the disproportionate impact this pandemic has had on children is even greater today. Experts in child development, mental health and education are all telling us that we must do much more to mitigate those impacts or we risk lasting harm to a whole generation of Ontarians.

Interjection.

The member for Renfrew–Nipissing–Pembroke will come to order.

Mr. Speaker, can I start again? Is that possible?

Please conclude your explanation.

The bill seeks to establish a child and youth action plan in collaboration with youth to address the ongoing impacts of the pandemic. It would also create a COVID-19 recovery youth secretariat to ensure that government bills are looked at through the lens of their impact on children and youth. A new standing committee will ensure accountability and transparency so that these issues are not just paid lip service but are measured and acted upon.

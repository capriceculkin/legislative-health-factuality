Three syntactic parses, every clause

Full run ready

Each sentence is parsed three ways; the same extraction rules turn each parse into subject | verb | object lines.
ParseTool and modelWhy this oneSample, lines found
Dependency 1spaCy 3.8, en_core_web_lg / fr_core_news_lgrules first built on it; fast; its parts of speech also feed stage 6103/119 en, 88/116 fr
Dependency 2Stanza 1.15different model and training data, so it fails on different sentences; best single French parser100/119 en, 95/116 fr
ConstituencyStanza (English); Berkeley parser benepar_fr2 (French)phrase boundaries, so noun phrases stay whole past commas; Stanza has no French model102/119 en, 77/116 fr

What the rules do

  • every clause, not only the main verb ("tried to set up", not "tried")
  • inherited subjects, marked (inh): "The bill (inh) | requiring | women"
  • appositions as their own line ("Brian Smith | is | the much-admired Ottawa sportscaster")
  • negation, and hedges: would, could, might, may, the French conditional; should, must, can, doit, peut are not hedges
  • stated, embedded (if, when, purpose, relative clause) or reported (someone else's words)
  • questions give no claim

Scripts

  • parse_corpus_v5.py runs all parses on the corpus, restartable
  • preds.py rules for dependency and Stanza trees
  • const_fr.py rules for French Treebank trees
  • benepar_fix.py runs the Berkeley parser on current PyTorch
  • run_compare.py the sample's saved parses (pc.json)

Discourse parse: stated, embedded or reported

Full run ready

Re-reads only lines the syntax calls stated. Sample: stated-or-not right for 102/107 English and 84/93 French (syntax alone 102 and 85).

Tool

IsaNLP UniRST (Chistova 2025): one multilingual model, RST-DT relations for English, ANNODIS (SDRT) relations for French. Runs on sentences with two or more predications or a speech verb (about 64%).

rst_run.py (sample) writes discourse.json; rules in combine_lib.py (edu_roles, rst_kind)

Rules that change a label

  • attribution, someone else is the source: reported
  • attribution, the line is the content of "ensuring that": embedded
  • condition: embedded
  • temporal, circumstance, frame starting with when, after, lorsque: embedded
  • purpose starting with to, pour, so that: embedded
  • "I think", "vous savez": the speaker's own framing, no change

What it changed

Sample: 1 line, and that change was wrong (Quebec sentence 83). Random 900: 75 lines (US 14, Ontario 17, Quebec 44), 50 of them through the "complement of an attribution verb" rule.

Decision for David: keep it as the judge, use it only for attribution, use its relations to link claims to their reasons, or drop it (saves about 29 of the 88 hours).

It will not reduce the surgery wait-lists that we have. It will not improve the quality of care that young people, children have, that seniors have, that adults have. What it will do is it will make it easier for investors and companies in Canada and in the United States to profit from Ontarians, because we have seen time and time again when independent, for-profit facilities set up shop in the United States and in Canada and in Ontario that there is additional billing that takes place.

The goal basically is twofold: to reduce and hopefully completely prevent the exploitation of students in the workplace, and also to ensure that they are safe in the workplace, that their safety is protected in the workplace. This legislation gives the employer clear direction about how to communicate the rights of student workers in the workplace. The onus is on the employer.

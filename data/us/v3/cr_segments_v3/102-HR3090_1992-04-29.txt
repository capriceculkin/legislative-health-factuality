VOTE TO REPEAL THE GAG RULE

(Mrs. MORELLA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1

minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)

Mrs. MORELLA. Mr. Speaker, we must vote to repeal the gag rule immediately. The gag rule .is an outrageous infringement of our first amendment rights. It will lead to defensive medicine, and it will create a two-tiered heal th care system. The gag rule will create a class system for women's heal th by denying poor women full information about their legal reproductive options, while still providing complete information and access to health services for women who can afford private physician care. The gag rule is patronizing to women, and it must be repealed. I urge my colleagues to vote for H.R. 3090, the family planning amendments, when the bill is considered on the House floor tomorrow.

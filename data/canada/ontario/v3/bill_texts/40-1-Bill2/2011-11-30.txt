The growth in CCAC expenditures has averaged 7.2% per year over the past eight years. The government’s forecast for the 2011-12 [to] 2013-14 period assumes that growth in CCAC expenditures will average 2.3% per year, or only about one third of the past growth rate.” Now, the obvious question to ask is, if the government is going to increase home care services, what number are they starting with?

On motion by Mrs Grier, the following bill was given first reading: Bill 119, An Act to prevent the Provision of Tobacco to Young Persons and to regulate its Sale and Use by Others / Projet de loi 119, Loi visant à empêcher la fourniture de tabac aux jeunes et à en réglementer la vente et l'usage par les autres.

This is far-reaching legislation designed to prevent illness and death and to promote health. I appreciate the comments from members in other parties today to the effect that they support it, and I look forward to its debate.

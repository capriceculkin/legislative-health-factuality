Out of 60 recommendations, recommendation number 27 reads as follows, "The Ministry of Health should study existing mental health legislation including the Health Care Consent Act to consider whether or not legislative changes are required to permit compulsory treatment of individuals with diagnosed mental illness living in the community who do not have the capacity to consent to treatment."

Mrs Grier moved first reading of Bill 135, An Act to amend the Health Protection and Promotion Amendment Act, 1983.

The House divided on Mrs Grier’s motion, which was agreed to on the following vote:

J., Stoner, Sullivan, Tatham, Villeneuve, Ward, Wong.

I appreciate the strong support from government members for the introduction of this bill. It is a bill that would prohibit the sale of irradiated food and food that contains ingredients that have been irradiated, and I look forward to second and third reading.

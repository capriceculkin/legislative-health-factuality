Mr. Harris moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 93, An Act to proclaim the month of April as Testicular Cancer Awareness Month / Projet de loi 93, Loi visant à proclamer le mois d’avril Mois de la sensibilisation au cancer du testicule.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

The member for a short statement.

Today I’m pleased to introduce the Testicular Cancer Awareness Month Act, 2015. The bill proclaims the month of April each year as Testicular Cancer Awareness Month to bring awareness and education about the serious impacts of testicular cancer and the importance of early detection and treatment.

Mr Gerretsen moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 91, An Act to amend the Highway Traffic Act respecting Paramedics / Projet de loi 91, Loi modifiant le Code de la route en ce qui a trait aux auxiliaires médicaux.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

The bill amends the Highway Traffic Act to allow paramedics to use lamps that produce intermittent flashes of green light on their own vehicles when proceeding to an emergency.

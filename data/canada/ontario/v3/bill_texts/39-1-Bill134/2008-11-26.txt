Mr. Kormos moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 134, An Act to provide for the election of members of the board of trustees of the Niagara Health System / Projet de loi 134, Loi prévoyant l'élection des membres du conseil d'administration du Système de santé de Niagara.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

The member for a short statement.

The bill provides that at least 12 of the trustees of the Niagara Health System are to be elected to represent the area municipalities of the regional municipality of Niagara.

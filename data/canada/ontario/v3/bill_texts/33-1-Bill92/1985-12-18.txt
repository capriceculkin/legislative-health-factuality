Mr. D. S. Cooke moved, seconded by Ms. Gigantes, first reading of Bill 92, An Act to amend the Nursing Homes Act.

This amendment would build financial accountability into the system by having financial information presented to the Minister of Health and then tabled in the Legislature for the scrutiny of all members. The public has a right to expect its money is going for care, not for profits, and if this bill is adopted, we can examine how money is spent in the nursing home system.

Mr Kwinter moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 126, An Act to amend the Medicine Act, 1991 / Projet de loi 126, Loi modifiant la Loi de 1991 sur les médecins.

Is it the wish of this House that the motion carry? It is carried.

Mr Speaker, am I not entitled to a brief statement?

The Chair recognizes the member for Wilson Heights for a statement.

This bill ensures that physicians who provide non-traditional therapies or alternative forms of medicine are not found guilty of professional misconduct or incompetence unless there is evidence that proves that the therapy poses a greater risk to a patient's health than the traditional or prevailing practice.

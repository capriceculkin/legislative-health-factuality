On motion by Mrs Grier, the following bill was given first reading: Bill 50, An Act to implement the government's Expenditure Control Plan and, in that connection, to Amend the Health Insurance Act and the Hospital Labour Disputes Arbitration Act / Loi visant à mettre en oeuvre le Plan de contrôle des dépenses du gouvernement et modifiant la Loi sur l'assurance-santé et la Loi sur l'arbitrage des conflits de travail dans les hôpitaux.

This legislation enables the implementation of the Ministry of Health's expenditure control plan. It is entirely consistent with the expenditure control and reform agenda of the ministry. The guiding policy principles have been to preserve medicare by ensuring its affordability, accessibility and the quality of care and payment only for medically necessary services.

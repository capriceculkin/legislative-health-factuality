Mr. Sterling moved first reading of Bill 215, An Act to amend Certain Acts Concerning the Sale of Tobacco to Minors.

Motion agreed to.

This bill puts some bite into a toothless law we now have in Ontario. It increases fines from a range of $2 to $50 to $200 to $5,000 for an offence in selling tobacco to a minor. It also introduces a measure of controlling the sale of tobacco to minors through the utilization of our vendor permit system under the Retail Sales Tax Act.

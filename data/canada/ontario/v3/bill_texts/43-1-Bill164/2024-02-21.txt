Madame Gélinas moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 164, An Act to proclaim Massage Therapy Day / Projet de loi 164, Loi proclamant le Jour de la massothérapie.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

I would like to invite the member to briefly explain her bill, if she wishes to do so.

The bill is quite simple. It proclaims the third Wednesday in October in each year as Massage Therapy Day, so we can recognize those professionals who help people every single day.

I have a further petition addressed to the Legislature of Ontario. "Say no to privatization of health care. "To the Legislative Assembly of Ontario: "Whereas we are concerned about the quality of health care in Ontario; "Whereas we do not believe health care should be for sale; "Whereas the Mike Harris government is taking steps to allow profit-driven companies to provide health care services in Ontario; "Whereas we won't stand for profits over people; "We, the undersigned, petition the Legislative Assembly of Ontario as follows: "Do not privatize our health care services." I concur and I will affix my signature to it.

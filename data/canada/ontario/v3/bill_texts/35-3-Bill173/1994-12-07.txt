Deferred vote on the motion for third reading of Bill 173, An Act respecting Long-Term Care / Projet de loi 173, Loi concernant les soins de longue durée.

This will be a five-minute bell. Call in the members.

The division bells rang from 1533 to 1538.

Will the members please take their seats. Mrs Grier has moved third reading of Bill 173, An Act respecting Long-Term Care. All those in favour of the motion will please rise one at a time.

Mr Martiniuk moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 176, An Act to provide for some continuation of benefit plans of employees after the end of their employment / Projet de loi 176, Loi prévoyant une certaine continuation des régimes d'avantages sociaux des employés après la fin de leur emploi.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried. The member for a short statement?

This bill amends the Employment Standards Act to provide that a person who has been employed for at least 12 months is entitled to have the employer provide, offer or arrange the benefit plan provided during that employment to be continued on an optional basis for up to six months when that employment ends.

When there is a settlement with that broader collective, whatever the benefits and wages that collective agreement and that collective action that they took comes to a conclusion, then those paramedics who remained on the job get the full benefit of that job action, and they will get whatever pay increases are arrived at, whatever benefit improvements are arrived at, and all the while they kept their essential services being delivered to the people of their municipality, which I think all of us in this place know is an appropriate and important thing to have occur. I congratulate the minister on a creative bill and a creative way of letting people retain their right to strike, letting them continue to be involved in a collective bargaining arrangement, yet protecting the citizens of Ontario.

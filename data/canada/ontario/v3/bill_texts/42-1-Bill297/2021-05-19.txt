Ms. Stiles moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 297, An Act respecting children, youth and young adults and the COVID-19 pandemic recovery / Projet de loi 297, Loi concernant les enfants, les jeunes et les jeunes adultes, et la relance après la pandémie de COVID-19.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

Will the member for Davenport briefly explain her bill?

Thank you, Speaker, and thank you to my co-sponsor, the MPP for University–Rosedale. The Centering Youth in Pandemic Recovery Act builds upon the motion I first tabled for debate here in March and calls on the government to recognize the disproportionate impact this pandemic has had on children and youth—from education to employment to mental health. This bill will establish a post-pandemic child and youth action plan created in consultation with youth themselves, and will create a COVID-19 recovery youth secretariat to ensure that the needs of children, youth and young adults are front and centre in every step of the government’s recovery plan. A new standing committee will ensure accountability and that our collective words result in real action. Speaker, a strong recovery for Ontario depends on the social and economic well-being of this next generation. With serious coordinated interventions, we can build a brighter future for all of us.

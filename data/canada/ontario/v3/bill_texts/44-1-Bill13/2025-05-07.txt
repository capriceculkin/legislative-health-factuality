Ms. Jones moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 13, An Act respecting primary care / Projet de loi 13, Loi concernant les soins primaires.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry?

First reading agreed to.

Does the minister wish to briefly explain the bill?

Indeed I do. The Primary Care Act, 2025, if enacted, its purpose is to establish the government of Ontario’s vision for primary care so that insured persons know what they should expect when accessing primary care within Ontario. The act sets out objectives respecting the design, the implementation and maintenance of the publicly funded primary care system within Ontario. The minister is required to prepare an annual report describing how the government of Ontario is working to achieve these objectives.

Mr Duncan moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 64, An Act to amend the Health Insurance Act to satisfy the criteria for contribution by the Government of Canada set out in the Canada Health Act / Projet de loi 64, Loi modifiant la Loi sur l'assurance-santé pour satisfaire aux critères régissant les contributions du gouvernement du Canada et énoncés dans la Loi canadienne sur la santé.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried. The member for a short statement.

This bill incorporates the principles of the Canada Health Act into Ontario statute with respect to health. In effect, it prevents any government from creating a two-tier health care system in Ontario. This government had a chance to vote against it once before. It's now part of our platform, and people will choose change.

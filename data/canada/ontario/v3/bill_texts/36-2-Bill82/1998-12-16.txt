There was no provision in the law for restitution if and when the ministry was able to find the perpetrator. Prior to 1995, the Ministry of Environment under my direction instituted a blitz in conjunction with the Ontario Provincial Police, the regional police and officials of the Ministry of Transportation in which they did a number of spot checks across the GTA and found a significant number of violations in terms of the transportation of hazardous waste, the illegal dumping of hazardous waste and so on.

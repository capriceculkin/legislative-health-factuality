Mr Kormos moved first reading of the following bill:

Bill 45, An Act to amend the Optometry Act, 1991 / Projet de loi 45, Loi modifiant la Loi de 1991 sur les optométristes.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

This bill amends the Optometry Act of 1991 to allow optometrists to prescribe therapeutic pharmaceutical agents for the treatment of certain eye diseases.

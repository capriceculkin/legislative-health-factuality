Congressional Record, Volume 154 Issue 121 (Wednesday, July 23, 2008)

REPORT ON RESOLUTION RELATING TO THE HOUSE PROCEDURES CONTAINED IN

SECTION 803 OF THE MEDICARE PRESCRIPTION DRUG, IMPROVEMENT, AND

MODERNIZATION ACT OF 2003

Mr. ARCURI, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report

(Rept. No. 110-769) on the resolution (H. Res. 1368) relating to the House procedures contained in section 803 of the Medicare Prescription Drug, Improvement, and Modernization Act of 2003, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

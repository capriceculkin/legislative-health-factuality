Mr. Fraser moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 16, An Act to amend the Workplace Safety and Insurance Act, 1997 / Projet de loi 16, Loi modifiant la Loi de 1997 sur la sécurité professionnelle et l’assurance contre les accidents du travail.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried.

First reading agreed to.

I invite the member for Ottawa South to briefly explain his bill.

The Workplace Safety and Insurance Act is amended to provide that an employer who operates a residential care facility or a group home is a schedule 1 employer for the purposes of this act.

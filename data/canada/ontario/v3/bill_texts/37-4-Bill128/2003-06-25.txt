Ms Churley moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 128, An Act to establish the Children's Health and the Environment Branch of the Ministry of the Environment / Projet de loi 128, Loi créant la Direction de la santé des enfants et de l'environnement au sein du ministère de l'Environnement.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried. The member for a short statement?

The children of Ontario have a right to a clean and safe environment. As children grow and develop, they are particularly vulnerable to environmental hazards, so I believe there should be an ongoing means by which children are protected from those hazards, to ensure that they grow up healthy. What this bill will do is require the Ministry of the Environment to establish a children's health and environment branch by January 1, 2004.

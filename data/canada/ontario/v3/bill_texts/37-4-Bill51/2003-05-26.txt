Mr Bartolucci moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 51, An Act to amend the Occupational Health and Safety Act with respect to acts of workplace violence / Projet de loi 51, Loi modifiant la Loi sur la santé et la sécurité au travail en matière d'actes de violence au travail.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried. The member for a short statement?

This bill amends the Occupational Health and Safety Act to impose duties on employers, supervisors and workers with respect to acts of workplace violence which are defined to be acts of physical or psychological violence that persons commit in the workplace.

I’ll direct my question to the Attorney General. He knows I have a big interest in this bill. I want to ask him about the function of the Judicial Appointments Advisory Committee. Thank you again for the opportunity to address the change. It is an advisory committee; it’s right in the name: the Judicial Appointments Advisory Committee. The way that it currently functions is the Chief Justice will say we have a vacancy in place X. That letter then goes to the committee—that will not change. The committee will advertise for that location, be it—I will just pick on Windsor—and so they will advertise for Windsor. People will apply to Windsor. It’s very cumbersome and it takes a very long time by the time they do the interview and they do the recommendations back. The change is a pool system so that if they apply to Windsor or London they end up in the same pool. They only need to be interviewed once, and the committee can assess them as recommended or not recommended. If they’re not recommended, I don’t see the names. That will not change either.

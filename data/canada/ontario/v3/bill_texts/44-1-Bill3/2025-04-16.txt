Mr. Hsu moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 3, An Act to amend the Gift of Life Act in order to recognize organ and tissue donors / Projet de loi 3, Loi pour modifier la Loi sur le don de vie afin de souligner la contribution des donneurs d’organes et de tissus.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the bill be read for the first time? Agreed.

First reading agreed to.

Would the member like to comment?

This is a bill that I’m reintroducing from the last Parliament, and it allows the named recognition of organ and tissue donors. I just want to mention who the bill is named after, Robbie Sherren. He was a blood donor while he was living and donated his heart to bring new technology, heart-in-a-box technology, to Canada. It took some work to be able to recognize him by name, but his family is very proud of him. This is a way of continuing his legacy, by naming the bill after him. Thank you.

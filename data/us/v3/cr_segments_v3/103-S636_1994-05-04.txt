Congressional Record, Volume 140 Issue 52 (Wednesday, May 4, 1994)

REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT TO ACCOMPANY S. 636, FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC ENTRANCES ACT OF 1994

Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 103-493) on the resolution (H. Res. 417) waiving points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill (S. 636) to amend the Public Health Service Act to permit individuals to have freedom of access to certain medical clinics and facilities, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.

I have a petition to the Legislative Assembly of Ontario. "Whereas Windsor-Essex county was the first community to undergo hospital restructuring; and "Whereas the community supported the recommendations of the Win-Win report based on a funding model that included the expansion of community-based care; and "Whereas recent reports estimate that Windsor-Essex hospital expenditure is underfunded by approximately $122 per person; and "Whereas this represents the lowest funding per capita for hospital services of any community in Ontario with a population of over 200,000; "Whereas hospitals across the province have been forced to further reduce expenditures by 18%; and "Whereas these cuts have forced hospitals to eliminate emergency services in the west end of Windsor and other desperately needed services; and "Whereas the minister acknowledged that additional funding was necessary in high-growth areas; "We, the undersigned, petition the Legislative Assembly to call on the Minister of Health to provide the appropriate level of funding to hospitals in Windsor-Essex which would allow Windsor Regional Hospital to provide urgent care services for the west-end community and to restore equitable health care funding across Windsor and Essex county." I join thousands of my fellow citizens in signing this petition.

Mrs McLeod moved first reading of the following bill: Bill 162, An Act to provide for the accreditation of care homes, to protect the rights of tenants and to amend the Tenant Protection Act, 1997 / Projet de loi 162, Loi prévoyant l'agrément de maisons de soins, protégeant les droits des locataires et modifiant la Loi de 1997 sur la protection des locataires.

Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? Carried. The member for a short statement?

Very briefly, the purpose of the bill is to put in place a bill of rights for the tenants of care homes, and to establish a care homes review board that is empowered to investigate complaints and to enforce the bill of rights. The bill also provides for the creation of an annually updated registry of care homes. This bill was first introduced and debated in this House two years ago. It was defeated by the government of the day, which said they were going to introduce their own legislation. In the absence of that legislation having been presented, I felt it was appropriate to reintroduce the bill for debate.

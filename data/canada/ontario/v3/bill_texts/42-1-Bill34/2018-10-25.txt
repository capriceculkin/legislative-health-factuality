Pursuant to the order of House dated October 24, 2018, I am now required to put the question. Mr. Rickford has moved second reading of Bill 34, An Act to repeal the Green Energy Act, 2009 and to amend the Electricity Act, 1998, the Environmental Protection Act, the Planning Act and various other statutes. Is it the pleasure of the House that the motion carry? I heard some noes. All those in favour will please say “aye.” All those opposed will please say “nay.” In my opinion, the ayes have it. A recorded vote being required, it will be deferred until after question period today.

Second reading vote deferred.

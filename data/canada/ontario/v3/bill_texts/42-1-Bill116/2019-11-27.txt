We need to get people off the streets; we need to get them out of hospitals and we need to get them out of jails, which are really, really expensive ways of caring for them. We need to get them the mental health supports that they need, and we need to make sure we have the resources, the overdose prevention sites and the harm reduction methodologies to make sure that all of that can come to pass.

Mr. Dietsch moved first reading of Bill 123, An Act for Informed Choice by Patients.

Motion agreed to.

The bill establishes several administrative procedures governing the performance of abortions in Ontario. The bill requires that a patient be provided with information concerning the life condition of an unborn child, the risk that may result from the abortion and the social services available to care for the child before consenting to the abortion operation. The bill also provides that no physician or nurse shall be dismissed or disciplined for the refusal to participate in an abortion because of objections based on moral or ethical grounds.
